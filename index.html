Why we do it

When I was a young girl in India, I loved celebrating Kanjak Puja (signifies the power of feminine divine). On this special day, people honored girls by washing their feet, tying sacred threads on their wrists, and seeking their blessings. I was taught that girls brought good luck and prosperity.

But as I grew older, I saw a different reality. Many families preferred having sons, while daughters were often seen as a burden. Boys had better access to education and opportunities, while girls were left behind. I couldn’t understand why so few girls were in top schools like IIT. I knew that if given the same chances, they would succeed.

That’s why I have one clear mission: to help at least one girl change her future through education—just as education changed mine.

Through my charity, we provide:

  • Skilled teachers so every girl gets a quality education.
  • Sanitary products so periods never stop girls from going to school.
  • Scholarships and learning resources to support extra tutoring and provide study materials.

To every young girl: Education is your power. Use it to build the future you deserve. GO STUDY :).
